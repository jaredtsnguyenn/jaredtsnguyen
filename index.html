02 / How I Work

Great work starts with better questions.

01 — UNDERSTAND

Find the friction.

Talk to the people doing the work, map what happens today, and identify the bottleneck worth solving.

02 — ALIGN

Make the tradeoffs clear.

Translate ambiguity into requirements, priorities, ownership, and a realistic plan the team can act on.

03 — EXECUTE

Build, measure, improve.

Stay close to delivery, remove blockers, track outcomes, and keep adjusting based on what users actually need.

03 / Behind the work

A little about me.

I'm Jared, an Economics student at UC Berkeley who gets excited about taking a complicated process, understanding why it's not working, and making it better.

At Tesla, I've worked alongside engineers and suppliers on vehicle development. At Handbook AI, I've explored how AI can simplify everyday work. At Bluebridge Tennis, I've helped build tools that make operations easier for people on the ground.

My favorite part is talking to the people closest to the problem, turning what I learn into a plan, and staying involved until something meaningful ships.

Outside of work, you can probably find me playing tennis, photographing places I visit, snowboarding, or working with Berkeley Phi Beta Lambda's investment fund.
